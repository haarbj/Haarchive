## "Black Monday" (pp. 148-149) — Oct 12, 1998, the death and its immediate aftermath

- Severy died in a solo mountain-bike crash descending Flagstaff Mountain (the same mountain the team runs on regularly) on his normal commute route from his cabin. A police officer found the crashed, unattended bike and then his body among the trees. The book explicitly, honestly states the cause of the crash is unknown and unknowable (echoed again on p.153: *"Perhaps a car was coming... Perhaps there was some gravel... Perhaps a bug flew into his eye. Perhaps. Perhaps. Perhaps. It is a futile game to play."*) — **treat the cause as genuinely unresolved; do not speculate on it in any Haarchive use of this material.**
- **The team's and coach's immediate real-time response, told with real, specific detail — worth preserving as an honest, non-idealized account of an athletic program's actual crisis response, since this is exactly the kind of real, serious event a coaching-psychology resource should be able to speak to honestly**: Wetmore learns from police, personally calls Goucher directly rather than letting him hear it secondhand, then gathers the full team in person to deliver the news before it could spread informally — Batliner's own account of arriving slightly late and realizing something was wrong purely from teammates' faces, quoted directly, is a genuinely raw, real description of how tragic news propagates through a team in real time, worth preserving as an honest artifact rather than paraphrased.
- The team's immediate response: dismissed from the meeting, scattering in different directions; several, including team captain Reese, went for a solitary run specifically to process the shock (*"alternately sprinted, walked, and jogged while crying, screaming, and cursing"*). That evening, the team spontaneously gathered together (not organized by coaching staff) at teammates' apartment specifically to share stories, photos, laughter, and grief together. Batliner's own reflection on why this helped, quoted: *"Everyone who showed up left in a little better shape.
