## Dispatch log

- batch-02 done: digested `Lydiard Method Presentation.pdf` (29 pages) and `Lydiard First Steps 2018 (1).pdf` (16 pages). First file is Lydiard's own words from a 1999 Brooks/American Track & Field lecture-tour guide (prepared by Nobuya Hashizume & Steve Bobenhouse), distinct from *Running to the Top*, plus a separately-authored 21-point "Training Considerations" section explicitly credited to John Davies (Olympic medalist). Second file is a Lydiard Foundation beginner-course product (Hashizume & Lorraine Moller), not Lydiard's own words. Both very rich and largely novel: detailed 1917-1972 Lydiard biography/history (Mexico, Finland, Munich 1972), a full reverse-periodization scheduling algorithm with granular weekly templates, a marathon-day 18-point checklist, an extensive (partly dubious) micronutrient rundown, John Davies's own numbered training maxims including "anaerobic training is a fallacy for developing speed," and the First Steps deck's named "5 Lydiard Principles" framework, walk-break-phasing psychology, and a worked Karvonen-style target-HR formula.

  Australian schoolboys XC team titles, with real session numbers throughout.

Very rich: dozens of novel historical anecdotes (Bowerman's 1962 NZ tour in much greater detail, Cliff Young, Mr. Wills, Marise Chamberlain, the Nuremberg shoe-development story, Lydiard's own biography), named coaching cues ("TRAIN and NOT STRAIN," "Buses with Mini Minor Motors in Them," "PLEASANTLY TIRED"), full staged beginner schedules and interval-block schedules with real numbers, and confirmed-gap material (HITSYSTEM "Response-Regulated"/"Feeling-Based" terminology, Lydiard biography).
