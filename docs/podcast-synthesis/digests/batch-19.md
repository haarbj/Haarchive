(SCIENCE — asserted/clinical-experience based, not source-named) (POSSIBLE GAP — specific, actionable kinetic-chain diagnostic concept)

(PRACTICE, with a specific numeric threshold) (DISAGREEMENT with "just stop running" medical advice) (POSSIBLE GAP)
