# Post-Season Review

*You've finished the marathon — congratulations. Before we plan what's next, let's capture what this cycle taught you while it's fresh. There are no wrong answers here; the goal is to learn your own patterns so your next buildup is better than this one. Write as much or as little as you want — but do write it down. You'll thank yourself in a few months.*

## Reviewing your training

1. On which days did you feel your strongest in workouts?
2. On which days did you struggle the most in workouts?
3. When in the cycle were you improving the most?
4. Did this training cycle go better or worse than your previous ones? What was different?
5. Was the plan too ambitious, too conservative, or just about right for you?
6. Are there types of workouts — or elements of training — you feel you're missing?
7. How long did it usually take you to feel recovered after your biggest workouts?
8. Are you happy with how this training cycle went? Why or why not?
9. What are your weak points as a runner right now, and what could you do about them?
10. If you could make one change with the biggest impact on your next cycle, what would it be?

## Reviewing the race

1. Did you go into the race feeling confident? Why or why not?
2. Did your race-morning routine work? Would you change anything if you ran this race again next year?
3. Did you get in enough fuel before and during the race?
4. Did your fueling plan cause any stomach problems?
5. Were you prepared for the conditions — heat, hills, wind, all of it?
6. Did the race unfold the way you expected it to?
7. When did you struggle the most? When did you feel your strongest?
8. Do you think a few more weeks of training — or a few fewer — would have changed the outcome?
9. If you could run it again tomorrow at the same fitness, what would you do differently?

## What happens with this

Keep your answers somewhere you'll find them. When we sit down to plan your next cycle, we'll go through them together — your patterns (what workouts you respond to, where you struggle, what went right on race day) are the raw material for making the next buildup yours, not just a plan off the shelf.

*As you gain experience, you'll build stronger intuitions about how your body responds to training — and eventually you'll be bending and customizing with confidence. This review is how that starts.*
